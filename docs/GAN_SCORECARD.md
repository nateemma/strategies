# GAN scorecard — baseline

Generated by `GANs/quality/scorecard.py`. Spec:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-26-gan-parity-design.md`.

Fixture: 4000 rows x 24 **production** features (`FeatureNormalizer.include_list`
via `DataframePopulator` at `DatasetType.MINIMAL`, matching
`BaseNNStrategy.py:1266`), real OHLCV from 6 liquid pairs, 2025-01 onward.
Labels are forward-return terciles — a learnable proxy, NOT the gbb target.
40 epochs per variant, 300 class-balanced synthetic samples.

## READ THIS FIRST — what these numbers are and are not

**This measures RAW `generate()` output.** Production does not use raw output:
`balance.py` applies density (:322), discriminator (:342) and autoencoder filters
downstream. GAN_TODO §5 reported NNNC TabDDPM at sigma_ratio ~0.5 — that was
**post-filter** (the AE kept ~70%). This table therefore does NOT contradict §5;
it measures a different quantity. Raw output = model quality. Post-filter =
deployed quality. **Both matter and only the first is measured here.**

**Utility is a proxy.** `delta_val_mcc` = MCC(train+synth) - MCC(train) on a
held-out real split, HistGradientBoosting, fixed seed. It ranks variants; it does
not settle P&L. Anything it flags still needs the §5 powered paired-seed protocol.

**Metric meanings.** `clip band` = fraction pinned at +/-4 sigma in z-space (the
WGAN/CGAN/DDPM guard). `bound sat` = fraction sitting at the real per-column
[min,max] (CTAB's guard, `mlx_ctab_helpers.py:35`). They detect DIFFERENT
mechanisms; a variant can be saturated under one and read 0 under the other.
`NN ratio` ~1 is on-manifold, >>1 is off it.

## Findings

1. **TF is not uniformly behind — the gaps run BOTH ways.** WGAN-TF preserves
   joint structure far better than MLX (max dcorr 0.211 vs 1.141) and has much
   better marginals (worst dmu 0.229 vs 2.212); MT_WGAN-TF likewise on dcorr
   (0.559 vs 1.030). But on CTAB the MLX side is better on nearly every axis.
   "Bring Keras up to MLX" is the wrong framing for the WGAN family, where MLX
   is the weaker implementation.

2. **Both DDPMs show sigma_ratio ~3.9 and clip band ~0.96-1.0 on raw output.**
   Confirmed NOT under-training (400 epochs only moves it 3.97 -> 3.44) and NOT
   under-capacity (production d_model=256 gives 3.88 vs toy d32's 3.94). This is
   what the AE filter downstream exists to clean up — see the caveat above.

3. ~~Every variant has a NEGATIVE delta_val_mcc.~~ **RETRACTED — that was
   single-seed noise.** Across 3 seeds, 10 of 11 variants FLIP SIGN (see the
   3-seed section). The utility probe as built cannot resolve an effect this
   small. Do not use the utility column for anything until it is strengthened.

4. **CGAN has no MLX backend**; `resolve_backend(prefer_mlx=True)` silently falls
   back to TF. The driver records this rather than double-counting the TF row.

5. **Fit time spans 50x** (MT_WGAN-TF 103.7s vs MT_WGAN-MLX 1.9s) on identical
   data. Relevant to anyone running the TF path on non-Mac hardware.

## Robustness note — do NOT run all backends in one process

Alternating tensorflow-metal and MLX fits in a single process can trip
`MPSGraphExecutable.mm:3457: failed assertion 'Incompatible element type for
parameter at index 0, mlir module expected element type f32 but received f16'`,
which SIGABRTs (exit 134) and therefore cannot be caught. Non-deterministic: one
seed completed the identical sequence, the next died at the same TF->MLX
transition. Run one process per seed (or per backend). Rhymes with the
long-background-batch SIGKILLs noted in project_noisycoconut_infra: isolate runs.

## D0 — utility probe strengthened, and its floor measured

The probe now averages `n_repeats=10` PAIRED repeats (one split per repeat, used
for both arms, so the split cancels) and reports `delta_sd` plus a `resolvable`
flag that is true only when |delta| exceeds its own spread.

Validated on synthetic ground truth: known-BAD synth (random labels) reads ~-0.05
stably at every budget; known-GOOD synth FLIPS SIGN at k=1 and tightens to an
across-seed spread of 0.0002 by k=15.

**On the real variants the effect is BELOW THE FLOOR.** At k=10 across 3 seeds,
sign flips fell from 10/11 to 7/11, but `resolvable` is 0/3 for essentially every
row: within-run sd is +/-0.016 to +/-0.024 while the deltas are +/-0.001 to
+/-0.008. The effect is 3-20x smaller than its own noise, and averaging pulled
every variant toward zero.

**Consequence: utility CANNOT RANK these variants.** Differences are smaller than
~0.007 MCC. Rank on FIDELITY (which replicates to 3 significant figures); use
utility only as a coarse safety check — does a variant actively poison the
classifier. This amends spec decision D2, which weighted both axes equally.

Third independent line of evidence for the GAN_TODO §4/§5 information ceiling:
different target, different classifier, eleven implementations, same conclusion.

## D1 — do the filters repair the defects?  NO (except off-manifold)

Raw `generate()` output vs post-filter (`filter_by_density` reject 0.2, then a
nearest-neighbour stand-in for the AE at reject 0.3), seeds 42/7:

| variant | σ raw → filt | Δcorr raw → filt | NN raw → filt |
|---|---|---|---|
| MT_WGAN-TF | 0.17 → **0.15** | 0.57 → 0.61 | 1.45 → 0.84 |
| WGAN-MLX | 0.36 → 0.35 | 1.14 → 1.14 | 1.58 → 1.52 |
| MT_CTAB_GAN-TF | 0.95 → 0.93 | 1.08 → 1.15 | 2.07 → 1.87 |
| CTAB_GAN-TF | 0.83 → 0.81 | 1.03 → 0.92 | 1.79 → 1.61 |
| MT_DDPM-MLX | 3.96 → 3.97 | 1.12 → 0.97 | 7.36 → 7.25 |
| WGAN-TF | 0.78 → 0.79 | 0.21 → 0.26 | 1.04 → 0.99 |

- **Under-dispersion SURVIVES and slightly WORSENS.** The filters are REJECTION
  mechanisms: rejecting outliers narrows an already-narrow distribution. Rejection
  cannot add variance that was never generated.
- **Joint-structure loss SURVIVES untouched.** A filter cannot repair a
  correlation structure that is wrong across the whole population.
- **Off-manifold-ness IS repaired** (NN drops 5-40%) — that is what the AE
  targets, and it is therefore already handled downstream.

**This explains GAN_TODO §5's AE-off > AE-on result.** §5 recorded it as an
operating-point flip; the mechanism is that the AE filter narrows an already
under-dispersed distribution. Every GAN in this family has sigma_ratio < 1, so the
filter is counterproductive on dispersion while helping only on manifold.

**Phase F scope, now evidence-backed:** fix under-dispersion and joint structure
generator-side; DROP off-manifold from the list (filters handle it).

CAVEAT: the AE filter here is a nearest-neighbour STAND-IN. The real
`filter_by_autoencoder` needs a pretrained artifact from a CreateAutoencoderFilter
run. Re-verify against the real filter before acting on the AE-specific reading.

## D2 — WGAN-MLX joint-structure failure (root cause OPEN)

WGAN-MLX is the widest gap in the table: max Δcorr ~1.24 vs WGAN-TF's ~0.21, on
identical data and a matched MLP architecture. One real bug was found and fixed;
it did NOT close the gap.

### Bug found and fixed (commit `ac70690`)

The MLX checkpoint selector ranked epochs by `abs(d_loss) + abs(g_loss)`. In
WGAN-GP `g_loss = -E[critic(fake)]`, so an improving generator moves MORE
NEGATIVE and `abs()` scores it as worse. Measured: G moved -0.018 → -0.98 over
30 epochs while the selector restored epoch 6 (G=-0.0126) — it reliably threw
training away. The checkpoint now snapshots the last non-divergent epoch and
restores only on early stop; `abs()` is kept for divergence detection, where it
is correct.

### Effect of the fix on fidelity: NONE (3 seeds, 42/7/13)

| variant | σ pre → post | max Δcorr pre → post | post sd |
|---|---|---|---|
| WGAN-MLX | 0.426 → 0.382 | 1.246 → **1.241** | 0.144 |
| MT_WGAN-MLX | 0.515 → 0.548 | 0.956 → 1.021 | 0.116 |

Δcorr is unchanged well inside seed noise. **The fix is correct on its own merits
— it stopped discarding training — but it is not the cause of the gap.**

NOTE: non-WGAN **MLX** rows also shift slightly post-fix (TAB_DDPM Δcorr
0.769→0.591) while every **TF** row is byte-identical. The fix touched only
`df_wgan_mlx.py`; the MLX moves are shared-global-RNG drift from WGAN now running
its full epoch budget. Treat them as re-draws, not effects. MT_WGAN-**TF** is also
not reproducible run-to-run (0.571→0.638 with no TF code change).

### Hypotheses tested and REFUTED

Five candidate causes were measured and eliminated. None is the cause:

1. **Checkpoint selection** — fixed above; Δcorr unchanged.
2. **`gp_weight` too high (50)** — swept 0.0/0.05/0.2/1.0/50.0. At `gp_weight=0`,
   with the gradient penalty entirely disabled, Δcorr is still 1.145.
3. **GP dominating the loss** — confirmed true (GP contributes ~50 vs Wasserstein
   0.36, i.e. Wasserstein is 0.71% of critic loss) but NOT causal, per (2).
4. **Architecture (CNN vs MLP)** — WGAN-TF forced to `architecture=mlp` still
   reaches Δcorr 0.239. Architecture is not the discriminator.
5. **TTUR / critic capacity** — no trajectory change.

Also measured, non-causal: the critic's input-gradient norm is ~0.0013, ~800x
below the penalty's target of 1.0, so `gp` sits pinned at ~0.997. The critic is
NOT dead — `critic(x)` mean +0.041 vs `critic(2x)` mean -0.014, and w_loss
improves (E[C(real)] 1.17 vs E[C(fake)] 0.68). It discriminates; the generator
just does not convert that into joint structure.

**Status: root cause unfound.** The defect is generator-side conversion of a
working critic signal, not the critic, the penalty, or the architecture. Do not
re-test the five items above.

## D2 RESOLVED (partly) — the missing term was class-mean supervision

Found by diffing the TF and MLX **generator losses**, after the five model-side
hypotheses above were refuted.

`df_wgan_gp.py:1229` adds a class-mean matching term to the TF generator loss.
`df_wgan_mlx.py` had no equivalent: its `loss_gen` was `-mean(critic(fake))` and
nothing else. So the MLX generator's ONLY conditioning signal was the critic —
and the critic's Wasserstein term is ~0.7% of its loss, the rest being the
gradient penalty. Class placement therefore never got learned. The fingerprint
was in the table the whole time: MLX `worst Δμ/σ` ~2.9-3.5 vs TF ~0.2, a pure
mean-placement failure, not a capacity or optimisation failure.

### The form matters: match the BATCH mean, not each sample

TF reduces over a seq axis before comparing (`reduce_mean(fake_x, axis=1)`). At
seq_len=1 — which is what tabular use is — that degenerates into pulling every
sample onto its class centroid, which destroys dispersion. Measured, seed 42:

| form | worst Δμ/σ | σ_ratio | max Δcorr |
|---|---|---|---|
| none | 3.544 | 0.400 | 1.302 |
| per-sample (TF's literal form) w=2.0 | 1.562 | **0.180** | 0.986 |
| per-class batch mean w=2.0 | 0.277 | **1.171** | 0.707 |

**Porting TF's code verbatim would have made dispersion worse.** The moment form
is the correct one at seq_len=1. w=2.0 was the only weight stable on all three
seeds (w=0.5 and w=10.0 each regressed on seed 13).

### Result (scorecard, 3 seeds, `cond_loss_weight=2.0` default)

| WGAN/MLX | before | after | WGAN/TF for reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| worst Δμ/σ | 2.87 | **0.18** | 0.24 |
| σ_syn/σ_real | 0.382 | **1.108** | 0.785 |
| max Δcorr | 1.241 | **0.781** | 0.224 |
| NN ratio | 2.402 | **1.608** | 1.029 |

Under-dispersion is GONE (0.38 → 1.11) and mean placement is now the best in the
table. **This also removes WGAN-MLX from the "filters can't fix under-dispersion"
problem recorded above** — that finding was measured on the broken generator.

### Still open

`max Δcorr` 0.78 vs TF's 0.22. Joint structure improved 37% but is not fixed;
the remaining gap is genuine joint-distribution learning, no longer confounded by
mean placement. Utility (Δval_mcc) remains below the probe's resolution floor, so
**no trading claim is made here — this is a fidelity result only.**

MT_WGAN-MLX has no class-mean term either. It supervises conditioning through
multi-task aux heads instead, which is consistent with its intermediate profile
(Δμ ~1.0 vs single-task's ~2.9, but σ still 0.55). Untested there.

## MT_WGAN-MLX — same missing term, plus a real scale mismatch

MT_WGAN-MLX showed the same fingerprint as the single-task path (σ ~0.52,
Δμ ~1.2). Two defects, and they only pay off TOGETHER.

### 1. The critic compared two different spaces

`_postprocess` de-normalises z-scored output back to raw space and clips to the
real [min,max]. It was applied to `fake_x` inside BOTH training losses, while
`real_x` is z-scored. Measured at step 0:

| tensor the critic sees | mean | std | min | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| real_x | 0.006 | 1.002 | -4.000 | 4.000 |
| fake (post-`_postprocess`) | 0.043 | 0.289 | -0.999 | 1.000 |

The critic could separate real from fake on magnitude alone. `_postprocess`
belongs on the OUTPUT path (`generate`) only, which is what the surrounding
comment always said it was for.

### 2. No class-mean supervision

The aux task heads supervise CLASSIFIABILITY, not PLACEMENT, so — as in the
single-task path — nothing told the generator where each class sits.

### They are not independent (3 seeds)

| variant | worst Δμ/σ | σ_ratio | max Δcorr |
|---|---|---|---|
| current | 0.997/0.983/0.911 | 0.50/0.57/0.57 | 0.954/0.868/1.182 |
| remove `_postprocess` ONLY | 0.894/1.021/0.841 | 0.43/0.41/0.49 | 1.124/1.027/1.043 |
| class-mean ONLY (raw space) | 0.828/0.786/0.939 | 0.77/0.77/0.70 | 0.903/0.702/1.052 |
| **BOTH (z-space, w=10)** | **0.219/0.202/0.207** | **0.96/0.92/0.88** | **0.532/0.630/0.611** |

**Removing `_postprocess` alone does nothing** — it looks like a dead end in
isolation. It only matters because it makes the target space coherent for the
class-mean term. Either fix alone would have been recorded as a weak result.

`cond_loss_weight=10.0` here vs 2.0 single-task: MT's loss carries adversarial +
aux terms, so the same relative weight needs a larger constant. Both were picked
for stability across all three seeds, not best-on-one.

### Result (scorecard, 3 seeds, vs the original baseline at the top of this doc)

| | Δμ/σ | σ_ratio | max Δcorr | NN |

**This inverts finding 1 for the WGAN family.** MT_WGAN-MLX now beats MT_WGAN-TF

## The same defect in the TF variants — found by prediction, not inspection

After the MLX work, MT_WGAN-TF held the worst dispersion in the table
(σ_syn/σ_real 0.175). Both TF WGANs already had everything the MLX ones had been
missing: class means, `cond_loss_weight`, linear output, and no `_postprocess` in
the training loss. So the missing-term explanation could not apply.

But 0.175 is almost exactly the 0.18 measured when the PER-SAMPLE form was forced
onto the MLX path. That yielded a prediction: TF's cond loss uses
`reduce_mean(fake_x, axis=1)` — a reduction over the SEQ axis, which at seq_len=1
is the identity — so TF has been running the variance-destroying form all along.

Confirmed. Switching both TF variants to the per-class batch mean (3 seeds):

| | Δμ/σ | σ_ratio | max Δcorr | NN |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MT_WGAN/TF | 1.83 → 1.60 | **0.175 → 0.652** | 0.614 → **0.431** | 1.353 → 1.315 |
| WGAN/TF | 0.19 → 0.17 | 0.794 → **0.899** | 0.233 → **0.193** | 1.038 → 1.073 |

MT_WGAN-TF improves on all four. WGAN-TF — already the strongest variant —
improves on three; NN ratio is marginally worse (1.038 → 1.073).

This is the one direction the original "bring Keras up to MLX" framing did NOT
anticipate: the defect was in TF, was inherited BY the MLX port in one case, and
was only diagnosable after the MLX experiment quantified what the wrong form
does. Neither backend was simply "behind".

## CTAB_GAN-TF — two separate defects; NO default changed

CTAB_GAN/TF and MT_CTAB_GAN/TF hold the worst joint structure left in the table
(Δcorr ~1.03-1.08) while CTAB_GAN/MLX reaches 0.37. Investigated; two findings,
neither yet actioned.

### 1. Model selection can restore epoch 1 and discard all training

CORRECTION: an earlier draft of this section called this universal. It is NOT —
it is seed-dependent. On seeds 7 and 13 `eval_quality` selects epoch 26; only
seed 42 collapses to epoch 1. It is a catastrophic TAIL, not the typical case,
and that is still reason enough to fix it (see the worst-case column below).

Verified in fresh processes (seed 42 — the collapsing seed, one process per row):

| epochs | σ_ratio | max Δcorr | best epoch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 0.839 | 1.066 | **1** |
| 40 | 0.828 | 1.033 | **1** |
| 100 | 0.828 | 1.033 | **1** |

Identical `eval_quality=0.5766` at all three budgets: 100 epochs of compute
return the epoch-1 model. Mechanism, from `ctab_gan_base.py:211`:

    overall = 0.4*diversity + 0.4*correlation + 0.15*statistical + 0.05*validity

`correlation_preservation` is `corr(real_corr_vec, gen_corr_vec)` clamped by
`max(0.0, ...)`. It reads 0.000 at EVERY epoch — truthfully, since the model
never learns correlation structure — so 40% of the criterion is a constant and
provides no ranking. Selection then falls to `diversity`, which is maximal for
an untrained generator (ratio 1.000 at epoch 1, decaying to 0.993). Epoch 1
wins, correlation never improves, and the trap is self-reinforcing.

Note the CTAB-GAN+ auxiliary losses are inert for the same reason: setting
`info_loss_weight` / `downstream_loss_weight` to 1.0 changes `g_loss`
(6.00 -> 7.14) but leaves Δcorr at 1.066/1.066/1.067 — the trained model is
discarded either way. `use_auxiliary=True` still builds and trains the aux
network, so that compute is spent for nothing.

### 2. Joint structure is bad at EVERY epoch — selection is not the cause

Changing the criterion does pick real epochs, but does not fix Δcorr (40 epochs):

| monitor_metric | best epoch | Δμ/σ | σ_ratio | max Δcorr | NN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| eval_quality (current) | 1 | 1.13 | 0.828 | 1.033 | 1.786 |

## CTAB composite FIXED — selection no longer rewards an untrained generator

Actioned after the finding above. `correlation_score` now derives from
`correlation_error` (mean |real_corr − gen_corr|), which falls monotonically as
the model learns, instead of `corr(real_corr_vec, gen_corr_vec)` clamped at 0,
which pins at 0.000 for exactly as long as the model has not learned joint
structure — and so stops ranking epochs precisely when ranking matters.

Applied to BOTH backends (`ctab_gan_base.py` and `mlx_ctab_helpers.py`), which
carried structurally identical composites. Keeping them in parity is the point.

### CTAB_GAN-TF, 40 epochs, 3 seeds (one process per run)

| criterion | mean Δμ | mean σ | mean Δcorr | mean NN | WORST-CASE σ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| eval_quality (was) | 1.033 | 1.016 | 1.062 | **1.961** | **0.828** (ep 1 collapse) |
| eval_statistical | 0.923 | 1.057 | **1.029** | 2.083 | 1.016 |
| **fixed composite (now)** | **0.823** | 1.111 | 1.063 | 2.052 | **1.101** |

Chosen on WORST CASE, not mean: the means are close, but the old criterion has a
tail where it throws away every epoch of training. The fix removes that tail and
gives the best mean placement. `eval_statistical` was the alternative — better
Δcorr, but it leaves the composite itself broken for every other consumer.

### Honest limits of this fix

- **Joint structure is NOT fixed.** Δcorr stays ~1.06. This repairs SELECTION,
  not learning. CTAB-TF still fails to learn correlation at every epoch while
  CTAB-MLX reaches 0.26 — that gap remains unexplained.
- **NN ratio gets worse** (1.961 → 2.052): the selected model is further
  off-manifold than the untrained one. Off-manifold is the one defect the
  downstream AE/density filters DO repair (see D1 above), so this is the
  acceptable side of the trade — but it is a real regression on that metric.
- Scorecard rows move little because it runs CTAB at 8 epochs, where there is
  barely any training to preserve. The 40-epoch table above is the evidence.

## CTAB-TF joint structure: LOCALIZED to the generator, cause NOT found

The remaining CTAB gap is not weak learning — it is active decorrelation.
Measured at 40 epochs, seed 42:

| | mean abs off-diagonal corr | corr-of-corr vs real |
|---|---|---|
| REAL features | 0.306 | — |
| CTAB-**MLX** output | 0.265 | **+0.976** |
| CTAB-**TF** output | 0.059 | −0.046 |

MLX reproduces the real correlation structure almost exactly. TF emits features
that are very nearly INDEPENDENT.

### Where it happens: inside the generator, before any post-processing

| stage (TF) | mean abs corr |
|---|---|
| REAL | 0.306 |
| raw generator SCALARS (pre-VGM) | **0.078** |
| final output (post-VGM inverse) | 0.059 |

The generator's own scalar outputs are already decorrelated. The VGM/mode step
only trims 0.078 → 0.059, so it is NOT the decorrelator. Mode usage is healthy
too: 9.79 of 10 VGM modes used per column (min 8, max 10) — not mode collapse.

### Tested and REFUTED as the cause

Do not re-test these:

1. **Model selection** — fixed (see above); Δcorr unchanged at ~1.06.
2. **Training budget** — 8 / 40 / 100 epochs all give Δcorr ~1.03-1.07.
3. **CTAB-GAN+ aux losses** — `info_loss_weight` / `downstream_loss_weight` at
   1.0 change `g_loss` (6.00 → 7.14) but not Δcorr (1.066/1.066/1.067).
4. **VGM inverse / per-column mode sampling** — both backends use deterministic
   `argmax`, and the split above clears the VGM step.
5. **BatchNorm inference statistics** — `generator(training=True)` and

6. **BatchNorm vs LayerNorm** (the one clear architectural difference from MLX,

7. **CNN backbone** — `use_cnn` already defaults to False; TF is on the MLP path.
8. **PacGAN packing** — already on at `pac=10`, the mechanism intended to let the
   critic see joint structure.

The defect is in what the TF generator learns, and none of the architectural or
scheduling differences examined explain it. CTAB-**MLX** is the working path and
should be preferred for CTAB use on Apple silicon; CTAB-TF remains usable but
does not reproduce joint structure.

## CGAN-TF under-dispersion — NOT fixable by moment penalties; and it is NOISY

CGAN/TF holds the worst remaining dispersion (σ_syn/σ_real ~0.44-0.55). It has no
class-mean term, so it looked like the WGAN case. It is not.

### Localized to the generator (postprocess is innocent)

| stage | median std |
|---|---|
| z-space real (clipped ±4σ) | 1.000 |
| generator RAW output (z-space) | **0.462** |
| final output (σ_ratio) | 0.454 |

Only 1.9% of final values are pinned at the [min,max] bound, and the output layer
is linear (`df_cgan.py:106`) — no activation ceiling. The generator simply emits
~46% of the required variance and the inverse z-score faithfully carries that
through.

### Two moment penalties added, tested, REVERTED

Both were implemented properly (wired through `backends/cgan.py`, default 0.0) and
neither moved dispersion:

- **Data-space per-feature std matching**, w ∈ {1, 5, 20}: σ 0.515 / 0.497 /
  0.510 / 0.514. Flat. The term IS active — Δcorr swings 0.824 → 0.657 → 0.901 —
  so the adversarial signal simply out-muscles a direct variance penalty even at
  w=20. Raw generator std moves only 0.462 → 0.487.
- **Class-conditional mean matching** (the term that fixed the WGAN family),
  w ∈ {2, 10}: 3 seeds, mean Δμ 0.450 → 0.493 (worse), mean Δcorr 0.797 → 0.866
  (worse), mean σ 0.437 → 0.475. Rejected.

**The WGAN fix does not transfer to CGAN.** CGAN's existing `fm_var` term already
matches variance — but of DISCRIMINATOR FEATURES, which does not constrain data
space. Adding data-space moment terms does not overcome the adversarial pressure.

### CGAN-TF is not run-to-run reproducible

The SAME config at the SAME seed gave Δμ 0.57 then 0.45, and σ 0.438 then 0.391.
An early single-seed read of the class-mean term looked like a win (Δμ 0.57 →

## CTAB-TF ROOT CAUSE FOUND — the discriminator is BLIND to joint structure

`GANs/quality/ctab_disc_probe.py`. Each trained discriminator scores three inputs
in its own encoded space: **real**, **shuffled** (every column independently
permuted WITHIN each class — class-conditional marginals preserved exactly,
cross-feature structure destroyed: mean|corr| 0.306 → 0.017), and **fake** (the
generator's own output, decoded then re-encoded so all three arrive in the same
space).

    joint_sensitivity = (d_real - d_shuffled) / (d_real - d_fake)

~0 = the discriminator scores joint-destroyed data like real data, i.e. it cannot
see the defect. ~1 = destroying joints is as detectable to it as fake data.

### Seed 42, budget ladder

| epochs | MLX joint_sens | TF joint_sens |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 0.057 | 0.339 † |
| 5 | 0.252 | -0.003 |
| 10 | **1.153** | 0.014 |
| 20 | 0.943 | 0.029 |
| 40 | 0.751 | 0.075 |

† discount: at epoch 2 TF's scores are -0.018 / -0.021 / -0.026, so the ratio is
noise over a near-zero denominator, not sensitivity.

### 3-seed replication at 40 epochs

| backend | seed 42 | seed 7 | seed 13 | mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MLX | 0.751 | 0.572 | 0.677 | **0.67** |
| TF | 0.075 | 0.030 | 0.030 | **0.045** |

A ~15x gap with no overlap across seeds.

**TF's discriminator works — it just cannot see joints.** At 40 epochs it
separates real from fake perfectly well (5.35 vs 4.83) while scoring
joint-destroyed data as essentially real (5.3516 vs 5.3125 — a 0.04 gap against a
0.52 real-vs-fake gap). It is a competent marginal detector and a blind joint
detector, which is EXACTLY the generator behaviour observed: TF learns marginals
(σ 0.824 → 1.092) and never starts on joints.

**MLX's critic acquires joint sensitivity at epoch 10 — the same epoch its
generator's correlation onset happens** (corr-of-corr -0.05 @5 → +0.851 @10). The
critic learns to detect joint structure first, and the generator follows. That is
the mechanism, measured on both sides rather than inferred.

### What this means for the fix

The defect is DISCRIMINATOR-SIDE. No generator-side change can fix it — the
generator is never told joint structure is missing, so there is no gradient
pointing at it. This retro-explains why every generator-side thing tried was
inert: the CTAB-GAN+ aux losses (info / downstream), architecture swaps,
BatchNorm-vs-LayerNorm, and the budget ladder all leave Δcorr at ~1.06.

It also explains the pac result without contradicting it: packing changes what
the discriminator is SHOWN, but TF's D fails to use joint information even when
packed 10-wide, so unpacking it changes nothing.

Candidate fixes, discriminator-side, none yet tested:
1. An explicit correlation-matching term (feature-matching on the off-diagonal
   correlation vector), which supplies the missing gradient directly rather than
   hoping the D learns to supply it.
2. Diagnose WHY TF's D cannot learn joints when MLX's can, on identical encoded
   input — the two Ds differ in width/normalisation, and that is now a much
   narrower search than the whole pipeline.

Given the powered A/B verdict (WGAN fidelity repair bought ZERO P&L), option 1 is
the cheap one worth doing only if CTAB-TF parity is wanted for its own sake.
CTAB-MLX remains the working path on Apple silicon.

## CTAB-TF JOINT STRUCTURE FIXED — four compounding causes, 0.043 → 0.240

The previous section root-caused the defect to a discriminator that cannot see
joint structure.  Acting on that finding closed most of the gap to MLX.  Four
independent changes compound; each is opt-in and defaults are unchanged.

All numbers: `GANs/quality/ctab_corr_probe.py`, TF backend, 40 epochs, real
fixture, 2400 synth samples, one process per config.  `real mean|corr| = 0.306`.

### The ladder (seed 42)

| step | mean\|corr\| | corr-of-corr | mean_dcorr | sigma |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| as shipped | 0.043 | -0.047 | 0.316 | 1.092 |
| + correlation-matching term (cw=15) | 0.086 | +0.810 | 0.252 | 1.109 |
| + drop per-column Gumbel noise | 0.139 | +0.938 | 0.194 | 1.159 |
| + drop G gradient clip | 0.181 | +0.952 | 0.155 | 1.180 |
| + generator 256,512,256 | 0.223 | +0.951 | 0.116 | 1.220 |
| + LayerNorm (FINAL) | 0.232 | +0.944 | 0.126 | 1.089 |

### 3-seed replication (42 / 7 / 13), final config vs MLX

| config | mean\|corr\| | corr-of-corr | mean_dcorr | sigma |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FINAL (gl+LN) | **0.240** | **+0.945** | 0.122 | **1.091** |
| gen-width alone (BatchNorm) | 0.223 | +0.952 | 0.118 | 1.202 |
| MLX reference | 0.253 | +0.981 | 0.087 | 1.029 |

**95% of MLX on magnitude, near-parity on structure, marginals untouched** —
final sigma 1.091 vs the shipped baseline's 1.092.  LayerNorm is the tiebreak:
correlation fidelity is a wash against the BatchNorm variant (dcorr 0.122 vs
0.118) but it holds sigma at baseline instead of 1.202.

### The four causes

1. **No gradient pointed at joints** (the blind D).  `corr_loss_weight` adds
   `MSE(corr_offdiag(fake), corr_offdiag(real))` to the generator loss,
   supplying the gradient directly instead of waiting for D to supply it.
   Matched in DECODED space (where the metric lives), which needs a
   differentiable decode: `_soft_decode` replaces the hard `argmax(modes)` with
   the mode probabilities the generator already emits.  Exact for real data,
   whose mode block is one-hot.  Forced to float32 -- a correlation is a sum of
   ~1e3 products and fp16 accumulates it too coarsely to give a usable gradient.
   Alone: -0.047 -> +0.810.

2. **No capacity to express them.**  TF gave every column its own
   `GumbelSoftmax` drawing INDEPENDENT noise; MLX uses a plain
   `softmax(logits/temp)`, deterministic given the shared trunk (it defines a
   GumbelSoftmax class and never calls it).  A decoded value is
   `mean[mode] + scalar*4*std[mode]`, so cross-column correlation IS correlated
   mode selection -- and independent per-column noise destroys it structurally.
   Demonstration: feed 4096 rows of identical logits, noise-ON gives per-row std
   0.295 on the first mode's probability, noise-OFF gives 0.0.
   **Alone it is INERT** (-0.047 -> +0.012): capacity with no gradient to use.
   It only pays in combination, +0.810 -> +0.938 and magnitude +62%.

3. **The G gradient clip.**  Both losses are summed then clipped per-tensor to
   norm 1.0, so the update is a pure direction.  Removing it: 0.139 -> 0.181.
   (It is NOT the cause of the divergence cliff -- see below.)

4. **Generator capacity.**  TF's trunk is 2 layers `[256,256]` + BatchNorm;
   MLX's is 3 layers `256->512->256` + LayerNorm.  Width is the magnitude lever
   (0.181 -> 0.223); LayerNorm restores sigma discipline.

### Refuted here — do not retry

- **Warm-up ramp on the corr weight.**  Above cw~15-20 training diverges (guard
  fires, restores an untrained generator).  Ramping 0->target over 10 epochs only
  DELAYED it (epoch 4 -> 8 at cw=20, which is where the ramp reached ~16).  It is
  a hard threshold on the EFFECTIVE weight, not a startup transient.
- **Removing the D gradient clip.**  Was the leading hypothesis for D's
  blindness.  At cw=0 it leaves joint structure flat (+0.018 vs +0.012 baseline),
  so the clip is NOT what blinds D; and combined with the corr term it
  destabilises training (diverges epoch 4).  Keep `d_grad_clip_norm=1.0`.
- **Critic width** `[256,512]` (matching MLX): 0.182 vs 0.181.  Inert.
- **corr weight above ~15** by any route tried (raw, ramped, G-clip off).

### Still open

The divergence cliff at cw~15-20 is unexplained.  It is not either gradient clip
and not a startup transient.  cw=15 sits safely below it, so it bounds achievable
magnitude but does not block the fix.

### Method note

Points 2-4 were all measured as INERT in earlier sessions.  They were tested at
an operating point where no gradient pointed at joints and mode selection was
still randomised -- so nothing could have shown a difference.  Re-test
architecture nulls after a mechanism fix lands, not before
([[feedback_evaluate_interventions_at_powered_operating_point]]).

### Now the DEFAULT (2026-08-29)

`CTABGANPlus` and `CTABGANPlusEnhanced` now ship the fixed config:
`generator_layers=[256,512,256]`, `generator_norm="layer"`,
`corr_loss_weight=15.0`, `gumbel_noise=False`, `g_grad_clip_norm=None`
(`d_grad_clip_norm` stays 1.0 -- removing it is harmful, see above).  Stock
defaults at seed 42 reproduce the validated config: mean|corr| 0.232,
corr-of-corr +0.944, mean_dcorr 0.122, sigma 1.101.

This changes the generator's architecture, so previously trained TF CTAB models
would need retraining.  In practice nothing on disk is affected: every saved GAN
artefact in `saved_data/` is MLX (`*_mlx.safetensors`) and there are no saved TF
`.keras` CTAB models at all.  Loading is safe regardless -- `load()`
deserialises the whole `.keras` graph and restores `generator_layers` from
metadata, and an old `GumbelSoftmax` config without `use_noise` defaults to
`True`, preserving legacy behaviour.

### Scope

Single-task TF, plus MT_CTAB-TF (ported 2026-08-29, below).  The MLX CTAB
variants use a deterministic softmax and are unaffected.  WGAN / MT_WGAN / CGAN / TabDDPM /
MT_DDPM have no VGM mode heads at all (their only noise is per-sample latent z
and the GP interpolation alpha), so cause 2 cannot occur there.

## MT_CTAB-TF residual gap — SIX hypotheses refuted, cause still OPEN

MT sits at 62% of MT-MLX where single-task reached 95%. Chased it; did not close
it. Recording the refutations so none are retried.

**The phenomenon, isolated.** Budget ladder, seed 42, selection OFF so each rung
measures the model AT that epoch:

| epoch | MT cw=15 | ST cw=15 |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | 0.108 / +0.791 | 0.110 / +0.849 |
| 20 | 0.098 / +0.777 | **0.175** / +0.881 |
| 40 | 0.112 / +0.767 | **0.232** / +0.944 |

At MATCHED weight and byte-identical networks the two are level at epoch 15,
then single-task doubles while MT flatlines. **MT is not slower — it stalls.** At
cw=30 MT is even AHEAD early (0.133 @10 vs ST 0.069) before stalling. So the
defect is a plateau, not a deficit.

### Refuted

1. **Architecture.** Measured, not assumed: on this fixture both build the SAME
   networks — generator 366,600 params `[256,512,256]`+LayerNorm, discriminator
   749,825, `total_feature_dim=264`, cond dim 3, 24-column decode plan. The MT
   adapter collapses to one 3-class task.
2. **Downstream loss** (MT uses `binary_crossentropy` cap 50, ST
   `categorical_crossentropy` cap 10). `generator_loss_weight=0`: MT
   0.154 -> 0.155, ST 0.232 -> 0.228. Inert in BOTH.
3. **Model selection.** MT restores epoch ~13 of 40. Turning restore OFF is
   WORSE (0.146 -> 0.129, 3 seeds) — selection is rescuing a degrading model,
   not discarding a better one.
4. **LR scheduler.** MT's `eval_quality` plateaus ~epoch 11 so ReduceLROnPlateau
   halves LR twice; ST's climbs and never triggers. Looked causal, ISN'T:
   selection returns epoch 13 and the first reduction fires ~epoch 23, so the
   selected model never sees it — and the decline starts by epoch 20, BEFORE the
   drop. `reduce_lr_patience=999` is bit-identical (0.146/+0.858, 3 seeds).
   A plateau CAUSES an LR reduction; check the epoch ordering before believing
   the reverse.
5. **The cw confound.** The first ladder compared MT@cw=30 vs ST@cw=15 (their
   respective defaults) — two operating points, not two implementations. Redone
   at matched cw=15: MT still plateaus, so the effect is real. (Separately, ST at
   cw=30 is fully collapsed, 0.048/+0.197 flat at every epoch — past its cliff,
   independent confirmation of the loss-share finding.)
6. **Noisy per-batch correlation target.** ST uses class-balanced bootstrap
   batches (`rng.choice(..., replace=True)` per class); MT takes a plain
   sequential pass (`rng.permutation` then slices). `_correlation_loss`
   re-estimates the REAL correlation per batch, so its batch-to-batch variance is
   the gradient's noise floor — which would explain "learns fast, then plateaus".
   Implemented a global target computed once over all training data. **Inert**:
   MT 0.146 -> 0.146, ST 0.240 -> 0.239 (3 seeds each). Verified the path
   actually ran (`_corr_target` set, shape (276,), mean|t| 0.3064 vs real 0.306)
   before accepting the negative. Reverted — a batch estimate over 1024 rows is
   already accurate enough. Do not re-implement.

7. **Training-By-Sampling (the sampler itself).** Ported ST's class-balanced
   bootstrap sampler into MT's loop, grouping by the JOINT task-label tuple
   (reduces exactly to ST's per-class behaviour at one task). Verified engaged
   from the log (`3 label groups x 341 samples/group`). **Inert**: 0.145 vs
   0.146, and the plateau trajectory is unchanged (0.138 / 0.108 / 0.122 vs
   0.137 / 0.107 / 0.122).

   **First attempt was underpowered and nearly filed as a refutation anyway.**
   The stock fixture is PERFECTLY balanced (1333/1333/1334), so a shuffled
   sequential pass and class-balanced sampling produce statistically equivalent
   batches — only with- vs without-replacement varied. Re-ran on a subsampled
   1333/200/200 fixture (6.7:1, closer to production's ~5% signal frequency) via
   the new `--imbalance` probe flag: STILL inert, 0.164 vs 0.164, coc +0.877 vs
   +0.875. Reverted. **Check that a fixture can express the effect before
   believing a null.**

### Still open — no cause found

Seven hypotheses, seven refuted. MT's plateau after ~epoch 15 is real,
replicated, and unexplained. The remaining untested surface is the discriminator
side (D's updates, the GP term, n_critic) — the generator side, the losses, the
sampler, selection and the schedule are all now excluded.


## Backlog task 2 — CGAN dispersion: clip hypothesis REFUTED, penalty ceiling REAL

The task-2 premise was that CGAN's data-space std penalty read "flat at w=1/5/20"
only because `clipnorm=1.0` caps update MAGNITUDE, letting a weight rotate the
update direction but never enlarge it — the mechanism task 1 established for the
corr-weight cliff. **Wrong.** Implemented a real data-space per-feature std term
(the `fm_*` terms match DISCRIMINATOR FEATURES, which does not bound output
dispersion) plus `g_clip_norm` / `d_clip_norm` knobs. Both verified reaching the
model and optimizer before running — the CGAN backend reads kwargs with bare
`.get()` and silently drops unknowns.

2x2, 3 seeds, 40 epochs (sigma target = 1.0, real is z-scored):

| config | sigma | per-seed |
|---|---|---|
| w=0  clip=1.0 (shipped) | 0.547 | 0.548 0.549 0.545 |
| w=20 clip=1.0 | 0.573 | 0.568 0.575 0.577 |
| w=0  clip=OFF | 0.538 | 0.548 0.549 0.517 |
| w=20 clip=OFF | 0.571 | 0.580 0.559 0.575 |

**No interaction.** The penalty gains +0.026 clipped and +0.033 unclipped — the
clip is not what suppressed it. The CTAB result (removing the G clip gained
0.139 -> 0.181) is CTAB-SPECIFIC, not a family-wide effect. The "every TF variant
clips at 1.0 while MLX does not" pattern is real but is NOT a general lever.

Weight ladder (clip on), 3 seeds:

| w | sigma | mean\|corr\| | corr-of-corr |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.547 | 0.314 | +0.982 |
| 20 | 0.573 | 0.310 | +0.981 |
| 100 | 0.568 | 0.258 | +0.971 |
| 500 | 0.580 | **0.113** | **+0.262** |

**Sigma saturates at ~0.58**: 25x more weight (20 -> 500) buys 0.007, while joint
structure COLLAPSES (+0.982 -> +0.262). Pushing dispersion through this term
destroys correlation long before it fixes variance.

**Verdict: the scorecard's original conclusion stands, on stronger evidence** —
weights to 500 and both clip settings, not just w<=20. A direct data-space moment
penalty cannot overcome CGAN's adversarial pressure. The ceiling is intrinsic to
the approach, not the weight and not the clip.

### Correction to the earlier reading

The penalty is not literally "flat" — it moves sigma 0.547 -> 0.573 on 3/3 seeds
in both clip conditions. Small, consistent, and real, but trivial against a gap
that needs 1.0. Kept behind `std_match_weight` (default 0.0, behaviour
unchanged): a +0.026 sigma gain does not justify a default change on one fixture
when the defect itself is untouched.

### Newly noted: CGAN's profile is the MIRROR of CTAB-TF's

CGAN's joint structure is EXCELLENT — mean|corr| 0.314 vs real ~0.31,
corr-of-corr +0.982, better than the fixed CTAB-TF (+0.945). Dispersion is its
SOLE defect. CTAB-TF was the opposite (good sigma, no joint structure). Whatever
fixes CGAN dispersion must not cost correlation — and this penalty does exactly
that when pushed, which is why it is a dead end rather than an underweighted one.

**Where to look next for CGAN dispersion:** the generator's output
parameterisation. The output layer is linear with no activation ceiling and only
1.9% of values pin at the [min,max] bound, so the generator simply emits ~46% of
the required variance from its own score. That is the same shape as the MT_DDPM
"under-dispersion is baked into the denoiser" finding — post-hoc and loss-side
corrections both failed there too.
