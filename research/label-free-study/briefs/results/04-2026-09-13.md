# Brief 04 — RL in a pooled environment: RESULT (2026-09-13)

**Feature**: [`specs/012-labelfree-rl-pooled-env/`](../../../../specs/012-labelfree-rl-pooled-env/)
**Analyze**: [pre-implementation](../../../../specs/012-labelfree-rl-pooled-env/analyze.md) (clean on re-run)
**Verdict: KILL.** Pooled walk-forward reward IQM **−0.1301**, positive in **5 of 15 folds**.
SC-001 and SC-002 both fail. The policy is *not* degenerate — it participates fully and loses.

## Hypothesis

Path-dependent decisions the differentiable simulator can only approximate smoothly — discrete
stop placement, grace logic, "exit now vs wait one more bar" — may be better learned by a
method that **samples** them. If a policy-gradient method cannot beat brief 1's differentiable
policy on the same environment, the extra variance is not worth it and the family closes.

## Config and seeds

`research/labelfree/` at commit `d7b11f6`. Environment: `env.py`, gymnasium 1.3.0, PPO via
stable-baselines3 2.9.0 on CPU. One shared policy over all pairs, episodes of 30–90 days drawn
from each fold's **training** range only. Reward = position × log return net of fee, spread and
slippage on executed exposure change, under the 10% liquidity cap; **no entry bonus, no idle
penalty, no take-profit multiplier**.

Screen: f07, 2 action parameterisations × 10 seeds × 1M steps. Pooled: 15 folds × 5 seeds ×
1M steps, each fold trained on its own training range and evaluated on its own test range.

## Trials logged

**505 pre-registered**, every one written before its result. Audit clean, 0 problems.

## The two gates, and what they caught

Both passed — but only after finding three defects in the environment itself, which is the
point of a control that can fail.

| gate | outcome |
|---|---|
| **A — env ≡ simulator** | **EXACT.** 0.00e+00 on all five cases (opening boundary, still-in-position, cap binding, zero volume, random) |
| **B — the reward can be hacked** | **8.46×** turnover under FreqAI's shape vs the pinned ≥2.0× bar |

**Gate A rejected its own specification first.** R1 demanded bit-for-bit equality with
`sim.net_returns`; the first run failed all 200 paths at a uniform 7.2e-07. That is not a logic
error — **MLX's `log()` and numpy's `log()` disagree by ~5 ulps at float32**, and the difference
is already in `bar_returns` before any of the env's arithmetic. The tempting fix, widening the
tolerance, would also have absorbed the boundary errors the gate exists for. Split into a LOGIC
part (exact, rtol=0, with the simulator's own bar returns) and a PRECISION part (bounded at
5e-06). Recorded as R1a.

**Gate B caught two env bugs before it could pass**, both of which would have produced
confident, plausible, wrong numbers:

1. **Drawdown was computed on the shaped reward.** FreqAI's −1/idle-bar penalty breached a 0.40
   limit in *one bar*, so every shaped episode died after a single step and the agent never
   reached an entry bonus. The probe reported **zero turnover** — reading as "the reward cannot
   be hacked" when it had simply never been exercised. Drawdown now tracks realised economics.
2. **The exit term multiplied the exit *bar's* reward**, ≈ −cost, making every exit punitive; the
   agent learned to enter once and hold forever. FreqAI pays `pnl × 100` — the *trade's* pnl.

## This failure is not the degenerate one

R8 predicted the likeliest failure was "learn not to trade" — zero exposure earns zero, which
beats losing, and brief 1's six best-ranked configurations were all below the participation
floor. **That is not what happened here.** On the test ranges:

- **1 of 75** runs falls below any admissibility floor
- 9 of 75 show the "hold forever" pattern (exposure > 0.95, < 20 trades/yr)
- pooled exposure 0.711 and **312 trades/yr**

The policy participates fully and loses. That is a cleaner negative than an abstention, because
no gate had to be invoked to interpret it.

## SC-009 — the brief's actual question — is INCONCLUSIVE

Brief 04 requires beating brief 1's policy, not merely `NNNC_MLX`. **Brief 1 persists no trained
policy**, so its best configuration was re-trained here (`utility` / `penalty` / γ=0.5,
epochs=4 — sweep.py's actual value, not `TrainConfig`'s dataclass default of 40). Its numbers
are a fresh draw and are not brief 1's reported figures.

```
 seed     brief1   brief1_w         rl     rl_w     delta
    1    +0.0006      0.001    -0.1510    0.550   -0.1516
    5    +0.0016      0.000    -0.2643    0.827   -0.2659
delta IQM -0.2270   CI [-0.2571, -0.1830]   RL worse 5/5
```

**The delta is not admissible.** Brief 1's re-trained comparator runs at exposure **0.0004**,
125× below the participation floor — it earns its +0.001 by doing nothing. A delta against
abstention measures the comparator, not the arm.

**This is itself a finding about brief 1**: it reported `utility-penalty-g0.5` at mean_w 0.130
on f08; re-trained on f07 it collapses to 0.0004. Its own report already said *"every
configuration that scores well does so by not trading"*.

## R4 REFUTED — and the correction reaches brief 02

R4 predicted a **fixed** no-trade band would avoid brief 02's `band_hold` bimodality, reasoning
the attractor came from the band being **learned**. The screen refutes it:

```
continuous  churn  6 seeds  turnover 108.8-165.3  exposure 0.148-0.654
            frozen 4 seeds  turnover   4.5- 12.5  exposure 0.003-0.012
            gap 12.5 -> 108.8, NOTHING in between
discrete    turnover 8.0-124.7 CONTINUOUS, exposure 0.453-0.974, 0/10 degenerate
```

The bimodality belongs to the **hold-inside-the-band mechanism**, not to learning the band: the
target either escapes the band and churns, or never does and freezes. **Brief 02 misdiagnosed
its own defect.** Its observation stands; its explanation does not.

## Budget is not the constraint; seeds are

A learning-curve diagnostic (3 seeds, checkpointed to 3M steps) settled the budget before the
screen ran:

```
 steps   reward IQM   turnover IQM      seed spread
  150k      -0.1950         101.02       60.8-139.5
  500k      -0.0951          20.72        4.8-102.0
   1M       -0.1919          91.74       58.7-115.9
   2M       -0.1905          80.76       39.7-150.0
   3M       -0.1892          64.71       38.6-162.5
```

Reward flat from 1M (+0.0013 per doubling), at a **loss**. Seeds disagree 2–4× at every
checkpoint and **move in opposite directions** — seed 1 rising 91.7 → 162.5 while seeds 2 and 3
fall. This is the seed sensitivity Théate & Ernst and Gort et al. report, turned from a citation
into a measurement.

## Verdict

**KILL.** SC-001 fails (negative), SC-002 fails (5/15 folds). SC-009 is inconclusive against a
degenerate comparator. The family closes.

Per SC-010, the differentiable estimator is preferred — but the honest basis is *lower variance
and backtestable state*, not a demonstrated performance win, because no admissible head-to-head
was obtainable.

## What this changes for the next brief

1. **The objective is not the problem, and neither is the optimiser.** Briefs 01, 02 and 04 have
   now failed on it from noise, from a warm start, and by sampling. Three methods, one objective,
   same answer. The next brief should change the **information**, not the search.
2. **Seed variance dominates budget variance in this regime.** Any future RL work here needs
   ≥10 seeds before a difference between configurations means anything, and no step budget
   rescues that.
3. **The band-hold mechanism is bimodal** whether the band is learned or fixed. Do not reuse it
   without expecting two attractors.
4. **Brief 1's comparator is unreliable across folds** — reported mean_w 0.130, re-trained
   0.0004. Any brief citing brief 1 as a baseline needs to re-derive it, not quote it.
5. **The trial log still cannot satisfy SC-004**, four features running. Storing per-bar net
   returns per trial is overdue and belongs to the protocol, not to another feature's footnotes.
