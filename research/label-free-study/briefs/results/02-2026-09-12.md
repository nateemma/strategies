# Brief 02 — Label warm-start + P&L fine-tune: RESULT (2026-09-12)

**Feature**: [`specs/010-labelfree-warmstart-finetune/`](../../../../specs/010-labelfree-warmstart-finetune/)
**Status**: INTERIM — Phase 6 carry-forward runs are gated (no arm survives selection); Phase 7 close pending.
**Verdict: NOT RESOLVED (SC-009).** No arm beats **both** controls by the floor. Direction recorded below:
against the P&L control labels **helped**; against the label control they **hurt**.

## Hypothesis

Brief 01 left one ambiguity: was its failure the *objective* (net-of-cost realised return is the
wrong thing to descend) or the *optimisation* (the right objective, started from noise, cannot find
the basin)? Warm-starting the policy from a label-trained classifier and then fine-tuning on P&L
tests the second. Three arms: **A** warm-start then pure P&L fine-tune; **B** a blended objective
(label cross-entropy + P&L, sweeping the blend weight and the label threshold); **C** an auxiliary
*non-directional* target (forward realised volatility) alongside the P&L term.

**A third inert-control defect, found by the post-implementation analyze.** Purge and
embargo were never applied: every `Fold` carried `purge_bars=0` and
`labels.apply_leakage_control` — which exists, is correct, and quotes FR-014's rationale in
its own docstring — was never called from `load_panel`, which also discarded
`LabelTarget.valid`. Under FR-014 the affected arm-B and arm-C runs are VOID. `run_screen`
now asserts the applied purge and embargo are non-zero and refuses to run otherwise, so
this specific failure cannot recur silently.

This is the third time in this study a control has been present in the code, believed
active, and doing nothing — after the training-range ranking (60 runs) and the inert
`label_threshold` (15 runs). The pattern is the finding: **wiring checks pass while the
term never runs**, and only an assertion on the value ACTUALLY applied catches it.

Void runs stay in the log and count toward the trial total.

**A retraction.** The voided Arm B run produced near-identical numbers across label thresholds and
I reported that as "confirmation the two labels behave the same". It was not — it was the same
panel scored twice. The threshold is now proven live by a per-run assertion that the two panels
differ.

**Two defects worth naming**, because both produced plausible numbers rather than errors:

- `aux_vol_loss` computed `sqrt(var) + EPS`. That is finite in the forward pass, but `d/dx sqrt(x)`
  is infinite at zero, so the *gradient* was NaN. Fixed by moving EPS inside the sqrt; the
  regression test asserts the gradient, not the loss.
- `label_threshold` being inert is the second instance in this study of a swept parameter that
  changed nothing with no error raised.

### Re-run under the fix (`20260912T153710Z`) — bit-identical, and why

The re-run with purge and embargo applied and recorded reproduces the voided figures
**exactly on every seed**: `pnl_control` −1.1282/−0.7389/−1.0894/−1.1408/−0.2364,
`armC_auxvol` −0.1004/−0.1083/−0.0990/−0.1268/−0.1125, and identical turnover (647.9 /
105.3 / 15.7) and exposure. Identical results from a changed input demand an explanation,
so it was measured rather than assumed:

| | length | already discarded by | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| purge (train tail) | 48 bars | the chunk loop's **850-bar** unvisited remainder | never visited |
| embargo (test head) | 48 bars | `evaluate_arm`'s **96-bar** burn-in | subsumed |

`train_arm` steps `range(0, T - CHUNK - 2, CHUNK)`; with T = 139,105 and CHUNK = 2,880 the
last chunk reaches bar 138,255, leaving 850 bars the training loop never touches. The
purged region sits inside that tail.

**So no leakage ever occurred in these runs** — FR-013's protection was being supplied
incidentally by chunk arithmetic and burn-in. That is not the same as being satisfied:
change `CHUNK`, the panel length or `BURN_IN` and it silently stops holding, with nothing
to detect it. The fix makes the guarantee explicit and asserted rather than emergent, and
the re-run makes the arm-B and arm-C figures admissible under FR-014.

*(Prior text, retained: the training frame is truncated at `train_end`, so no data crossed
the boundary; the defect was the absent mandated record, not measured leakage.)*

Arm C paired against the P&L control: mean **+0.7573**, bootstrap 95% CI **[+0.4060, +1.0148]**,
**5/5 seeds**.

## Persistence — fold f07 (rising, buy & hold +0.5433 log / +72.2%)

f08 is a bear fold, so Arm C's advantage could have been an artifact of a falling market. It is not.

| | f07 (rising) | f08 (falling) |
|---|---|---|
| buy & hold | +0.5433 | −0.3830 |
| P&L control | −0.5257 | −1.0894 |
| **Arm C** | **+0.0934** | **−0.1083** |
| DO NOTHING | 0.0000 | 0.0000 |
| paired delta (C − control) | **+0.6191** | **+0.7573** |
| seeds positive | 4/5 | 5/5 |
| bootstrap 95% CI | [+0.2723, +0.9366] | [+0.4060, +1.0148] |

The deltas are statistically indistinguishable and both exclude zero. **The advantage is
regime-independent.**

*Buy & hold is quoted from the 96-bar burn-in, the same bar the arms' returns start on.
Measured from bar 0 it is +0.5279 / −0.4255; the burn-in-aligned figure is the comparable
one and is what `baselines.py` emits.*

**But the persistence test devalues the finding rather than supporting it.** Read Arm C's own
return instead of what it beats:

- vs DO NOTHING: +0.0934 (f07), −0.1083 (f08) — across both folds **−0.0075**, a wash.
- vs buy & hold in f07: **−0.4499**. It captured 9.8% of a 72.2% rally.

Two diagnostics explain it. Arm C's across-seed dispersion in f07 is **sd = 0.0345** against the
control's **0.4489** — a 13× collapse. And its mean exposure barely moves between folds (0.369
rising, 0.434 falling) while the market swings 104 points. It is not taking a direction; it holds a
small, near-constant exposure regardless of regime.

**So Arm C's edge over the P&L control is variance reduction, not alpha.** The +0.6 to +0.8 paired
delta is almost entirely the control's losses being *avoided*, not gains being made. The striking
number is the P&L control losing 52% in a +72.2% market — a statement about the control, not about
Arm C.

Arm C beats the TSMOM rule in both folds and the production control in neither. It does not

**A defect found here.** The protocol specifies "20/60-bar EMA **sign**, band ±0.1". Read as
a raw price ratio, ±0.1 means "fast exceeds slow by 10% of slow", which on 15-minute bars
with 20/60-bar spans never happens: the rule sat **100% flat on both folds**. A baseline that
never trades is not a baseline. It is implemented as vol-normalised time-series momentum,
normalised by the rule's own 60-bar span so no new constant enters, and a regression test
now asserts the rule actually trades.

## What the arms are actually doing

|---|---|---|---|
| `pnl_control` | −1.0894 | 0.447 | 647.9 |

| `armC_auxvol` | −0.1083 | 0.426 | **15.7** |

**Spearman(return, turnover) = −1.000.** The ordering is exact. Spearman(return, exposure)
is only −0.400, so it is not a position-size effect.

Arm C holds **the same exposure as the control** (0.426 vs 0.447, −4.7%) and trades it
**41× less**. It is not taking better positions; it is paying 41× less to hold the same
ones. The auxiliary volatility target produces a smooth, slowly-varying exposure, and
smoothness is what is being rewarded.

**So "labels helped" is not the right reading of the ordering.** The ordering is a turnover
ordering, and the thing separating the arms is cost, not signal.

## DSR / PBO (T037, SC-004) — CANNOT BE EVALUATED

Computed over the full trial log: **318 pre-registered trials**, 21 complete configurations,
5 seeds. Raw figures: DSR 0.902, PBO 0.000.

**Both are reported as not interpretable, and SC-004 is recorded as unevaluated rather than
satisfied.** DSR deflates a Sharpe computed over a *return series* and CSCV partitions that
series into blocks. The trial log stores only summary scalars per run, so the only axis
available is **seeds — 5 of them**. On that axis the "Sharpe" measures seed-consistency, not
risk-adjusted return, and a configuration that is reliably mediocre scores well precisely
because its across-seed dispersion is tiny. CSCV ran with 4 splits instead of 16, and 21
configurations is inside the documented biased-low regime.

Reporting PBO = 0.000 as a pass would be the exact selection error the deflation exists to
correct. Satisfying SC-004 as written requires **per-bar net returns stored per trial**,
which is a trial-log schema change and is recorded as the blocking item.

## The turnover-matched control — the confound, measured (2026-09-12)

Pre-registered at [`02-turnover-match-preregistration.md`](02-turnover-match-preregistration.md)
before any run, with two deviations recorded there as they happened.

### Four levers, three failures

| lever | acts on | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `gamma` | perceived cost in training | **FAILED** — 50× range moved turnover 19%, non-monotonically |
| `lam_turnover` | explicit λ·\|Δw\| in the loss | **FAILED** — saturates at turnover 0.1 from λ=0.005 up; λ=0.001 already at 2.2 |
| `turnover_control="penalty"` | — | **INADMISSIBLE** — selects a *different model* (no band head, no `band_hold` scan). Tell was a 12× speedup: 15s vs 183s |
| `band_width` | no-trade zone at FIXED architecture | **SUCCEEDED** |

`lam_turnover` failing was itself a defect: `arms.py` had no loss-side penalty branch at all
while `train.py:111` did, so `turnover_control="penalty"` silently gave the band with no
penalty. Fourth present-but-not-acting parameter in this study.

### Turnover is bimodal; the IQM still lands in the gap

The `band_hold` scan has two attractors — a band narrow enough to move nearly every bar, or
wide enough to hold the opening position for the whole window. At a *single* `band_width`,
seeds land in either:

```
matched control (band_width=0.5), per seed:
  turnover   33.3 | 545.0 | 15.4 | 0.0 | 0.3
  log_ret  -0.1745|-0.8963|-0.1674|-0.1123|-0.1575
```

An interim reading of the single-seed brackets called the gap empty and the match
impossible. **That was wrong** — it was made on partial data. The IQM across 5 seeds lands
at turnover 16.3 even though no individual seed sits near it.

| config | IQM log_ret | IQM w | IQM turnover | match vs Arm C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `armC_auxvol` | −0.1071 | 0.429 | 16.9 | (target) |
| `ctlbw_0.45` | −0.1350 | 0.484 | 0.1 | no — turnover |
| **`ctlbw_0.5`** | **−0.1664** | **0.564** | **16.3** | **ADMISSIBLE** |
| `ctlbw_0.55` | −0.1478 | 0.554 | 7.0 | no — turnover |

### Verdict: the advantage is 92% cost, 8% signal

| statistic | delta vs matched control | CI |
|---|---|---|
| mean | +0.1922 | [+0.0198, +0.4966] |
| **IQM (pre-registered, SC-014)** | **+0.0625** | [+0.045, +0.074] |
| IQM(arm) − IQM(control) | **+0.0594** = **+27.2 pp/yr** | floor 3.35 pp/yr |

Both routes exclude zero and agree on sign, so the conclusion is robust to the choice. The
magnitude is not: the mean is 3× the IQM because seed 2 alone fell into the churn attractor
and lost 0.90. **The honest figure is the IQM's +0.06, not the mean's +0.19** — quoting the
mean would be the outlier-seed instance of the error this study keeps finding.

**Matching turnover collapses Arm C's advantage from +0.7573 to +0.0594.** 92% of the
headline was cost. The residual 8% is real, resolvable, and clears the floor 8× — and it
belongs to a **non-directional volatility target that carries no label information at all**.

### Arm B cannot be matched

Arm B's IQM exposure is 0.125; every band control in the reachable regime sits at 0.48–0.56.
It fails criterion 4 (exposure within 2×) at *any* turnover. That match is unavailable, and
the criterion was not relaxed to manufacture it. **The label-prior question is therefore
unanswered**, not answered negatively.

## Verdict

**NOT RESOLVED against the study's acceptance gate (SC-009)**, and no arm is carried forward.
But the brief's own question is now answered, which is the deliverable SC-009 asks for.

- **No arm beats both controls by the floor.** Arm C loses to the label control (`NNNC_MLX`,
  −0.0014) by ~10.6 pp. SC-009 fires.
- **The "labels helped, monotonically" reading is withdrawn.** The A/B/C ordering
  (+0.56 / +0.96 / +0.98 over the P&L control) is an *exact* turnover ordering
  (Spearman −1.000), and the architecture regression that produced that control is
  −0.7707 log — 1.02× the entire advantage being claimed.
- **At matched turnover the auxiliary target retains +27.2 pp/yr**, CI excluding zero,
  clearing the floor 8×. 92% of the headline was cost; the residual 8% is signal, and it
  comes from a *non-directional* target carrying no label content.
- **The label prior itself is untested.** Arm B is unmatchable on exposure, so whether a
  directional label adds anything remains open.

The directional finding: **what helped was not trading less badly, it was trading less** —
and the one thing that survived cost-matching is the least label-like component in the brief.

## What this changes for the next brief

1. **Report against DO NOTHING and a turnover-matched control, never against a free-running
   P&L control.** The unmatched control loses 52% in a +72.2% market and 92% of this brief's
   headline advantage was the gap to it. Any arm that trades less beats it. This is now a
   measured rule, not a caution.
2. **The label-prior question moves to brief 03 unchanged.** Arm B could not be matched on
   exposure, so briefs 01 and 02 together have *not* tested whether a directional label
   helps — they tested whether descending net-of-cost return works (it does not, from noise
   or from a warm start). Brief 03 uses the trade archive rather than this trainer, so
   neither the architecture regression nor the turnover confound reaches it. It is the
   strongest remaining line.
3. **The surviving signal is non-directional.** The only thing that cleared cost-matching is
   a forward-*volatility* head. That is an argument for brief 06 (generative stress harness)
   and against brief 04 (RL on the same objective through the same trainer), which should be
   re-scoped or dropped.
4. **Fix the trainer before it is reused.** Two defects outlive this brief: a −0.7707 log
   regression on an identical nominal configuration versus brief 01 (turnover 194→648), and
   `band_hold`'s bimodality, which makes any turnover setting a coin-flip between two
   attractors rather than a level. Both are properties of the shared trainer that every
   remaining brief inherits.
5. **SC-004 needs a trial-log schema change** — per-bar net returns per trial, so DSR and PBO
   have a time axis. Until then no brief in this study can satisfy it as written.
6. **Liveness probes are now standing infrastructure** (`research/labelfree/liveness.py`).
   Four parameters in this study were configured, logged, believed active, and reaching
   nothing — three were caught only by asserting on the value ACTUALLY applied. Every future
   brief should run `check_all()` before spending compute on a sweep.
